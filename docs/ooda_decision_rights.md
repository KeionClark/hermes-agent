# OODA decision rights (dark P1 classifier)

`hermes_cli.ooda_decision_rights.classify(action_type: ActionType, context: ActionContext) -> Decision` is a pure, non-executing triage function. Its `Decision` is immutable and carries `outcome`, `reason_code`, and `owner`. Import `ActionType`, `ActionContext`, `ReservedEffect`, and `DecisionOutcome` from the same module. The 25 `ActionType` values are the architecture §6 action vocabulary; no free-form action label is accepted. A result of `agent_authorized` is limited to evidence-backed bounded internal work, not a release or a message; `dark_build_only` is expressly **not** permission to merge, deploy, arm, send, or claim a production witness.

The rule of precedence is OPERATING_POLICY.md §29–33 (dated 29–30 Sep 2026), then the Chief of Staff's DECISION_BRAIN.md (28–30 Sep 2026), over the older architecture's blanket Tier C labels. Classify the actual effect/audience/scope; a new bounded conversation step, booking-rule change, or same-purpose patient-table correction is not automatically Keion's decision. Chief of Staff may approve a complete bounded packet subject to independent QA, project ruling, approved pilot and hard send cap where applicable, ordered release and production read-back. This module records evidence references, not the truth or scope-match of a reference. Release Engineer and Quality Auditor must independently check the packet and the serving revision; the classifier cannot replace those gates. `production_witness_ref` only describes a past witness, never a future authorization.

All `ReservedEffect` members route to `keion_decision` for every action: new patient audience, bulk/above-cap sends, increased cadence, unapproved clinical/diagnostic advice, treatment/price commitment, material consent/opt-out change, new sharing/export/access/purpose, irreversible deletion or retention reduction, spending/purchase/billing/account/security/vendor commitment, and material conflict or an explicit project approval requirement. `bulk_send`, `increase_spend`, and `pivot_strategy` themselves cannot be authorized by this classifier. A bare `explicit_approval_ref` does not prove Keion approved this specific effect/scope; even with one present, reserved work remains `keion_decision` pending independent verification of the exact decision. Silence or elapsed time cannot convert it into consent.

Callers populate `ActionContext` from original source evidence, not an LLM verdict. Required for internal reversible actions: nonempty `evidence_ref`, `effect_scope`, `audience`, `same_purpose=True`, `bounded_pilot=True`, `bounded_safe_scope=True`, and `dependency_ready=True`; an investigation card missing effect or scope is `evidence_needed`. Explicitly absent dependency returns `dependency_wait`. Cron schedule/unpause, webhook routes and OODA-agent changes additionally require `opt_in=True`; even a fully evidenced proposed infrastructure change is `dark_build_only`. For live or staging/product actions additionally require `rollback_ready=True`, `qa_revision`, `project_ruling_ref`, `release_gate_ref`, and confirmed external access. Send-related actions require a positive integer `send_cap`; the release owner must verify actual audience, recipient, existing route, duplicate-send state, opt-out/human takeover and cap enforcement. Missing access after other checks yields `external_access_needed`; unknown access yields `evidence_needed`. Any absent, blank, unknown or contradictory safety fact fails closed. Never infer these fields from filenames, migration names, regex, elapsed timers or model confidence.

Examples: a bounded same-purpose practice booking fix with a complete packet yields `dark_build_only`, then follows the project's ordered gates and production witness; a new patient cohort is `keion_decision` even if that same packet is attached. A scoped investigation card with an evidence reference and bounded effect is `agent_authorized`; one with no effect scope is `evidence_needed`. A cron reconfiguration with no explicit opt-in is `evidence_needed`.
